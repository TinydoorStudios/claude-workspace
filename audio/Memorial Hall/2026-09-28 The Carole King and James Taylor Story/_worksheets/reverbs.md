# Reverbs — 7th Heaven Pro (KB) + CLA Epic (new, no KB)

Standing rule 2026-09-26 (Brian): 7th Heaven + CLA Epic are the ONLY Memo reverbs from now on.

## CLA Epic facts (Waves user guide v2, read in full)
- 4 delays → 4 reverbs, routable: Tape (2 taps 1–5000 ms, sync, feedback, offset ±20%, mod), Throw (Tape + manual Trigger gate), Slap (1–5000 ms or VSO 7.5/15/30 IPS with a tied EQ curve, feedback, mod), Crowd (one Tight↔Wide knob; growing early-reflection cluster).
- Reverbs Plate / Room / Hall: pre-delay 0–1000 ms, RT 0.1–20 s, RT Low and RT High multipliers (×; e.g. ×0.25–×1.5 for halls). Space adds Decay 0.04–3.5 (non-linear/gate).
- Every processor: fader −inf..+10, HP/LP filters on its output, mute/solo. Modulator = one knob, 4 depth zones × rate.
- Delays can feed any reverb (serial), output (parallel) or both. Reverbs → output only.
- Stereo and Mono-to-Stereo components. Wet path internally downsampled to 44.1 kHz. Sync follows host tempo.
- NO ducker (unlike 7th Heaven) → ducking must come from the host/console (sidechain comp on the return) or the Throw trigger.
- 50 CLA presets + 300 artist presets; names not published → settings written from controls, "(custom — no factory anchor)".

## Show fit
- Material: 70s singer-songwriter, two soft voices, piano + fingerstyle guitar. Records ("Tapestry", Troubadour 1970) were dry-ish, plate/chamber, slap on JT's vocal is era-true.
- 7th Heaven carries the "room/real" work (V1 per KB: acoustic → V1). CLA Epic carries the "record" work: slap, plate, throws.
