GROUP vs CH  Answer: split the job. High/low is NOT a coincident stereo pair — each mic sees a different register and different problems (Lo: bass-string boom + wound-string 250 box; Hi: hammer edge + the wedge/vocal bleed off the lid ~2–4k). Per-channel = mic-specific correction (HPF per register, Lo's box, Hi's bleed/edge). Group = the room and the tone: Memo standing waves and the overall "Carole King warmth" shape, applied linked so the image never shifts. Practical: the patcher writes input EQ only; group EQ is documented and dialed by hand. Recording is pre-EQ either way.
