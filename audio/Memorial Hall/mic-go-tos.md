## Notes

- **Kick combo:** Beta 52 (body) + Beta 91 (attack/head) is the standard two-mic setup
- **Guitar cab combo:** SM57 primary (CH13) + R-121 blend (CH15) at The Memo — R-121 sits 6–10 dB below 57; polarity-check in mono
- **Classical main pair:** MKH 40s are the first call; Schoeps CMC6/MK4 for spots and soloist coverage
- **Memorial Hall crowd rig:** All three pairs patch standard every show — EQ starting points in `venue-notes.md`
