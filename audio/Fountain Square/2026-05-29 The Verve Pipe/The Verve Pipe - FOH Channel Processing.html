Critical Notes
  • Polarity checks — BEFORE advancing levels: Ch 4 vs Ch 3 (snare bottom vs top) · Ch 12 vs Ch 11 (bass mic vs DI) · Ch 14 vs Ch 13 (Guitar Center blend) · Ch 16 vs Ch 15 (Guitar Right blend) · Ch 9/10 stereo overhead mono sum
  • Guitar dual-mic blends (Ch 13/14 and Ch 15/16): Start with the SM57 at unity and bring the Beta27 up 6–8 dB below. Check polarity on each Beta27 independently before blending. After polarity is confirmed, listen for comb filtering around 300–500 Hz when both are open — pull that band if needed on either channel.
  • Bass blend (Ch 11/12): DI owns the low end; mic adds grit and cab character. Polarity flip on Ch 12 is very likely — check before blending.
  • Outdoor gain margins: FSQ has a higher ambient noise floor than Memo. Build in more headroom on all channels. The L-Acoustics A15 rig is tuned — resist the urge to boost anything; cuts are more effective here.
  • Vocals: Not in this patch. Vocals are arriving via Dante/wireless on a separate route. Monitor the Dante feed before show.
  • No reverb: Outdoor setting at FSQ — no room reverb applied. If talent requests reverb on monitors (via M32), that's separate from FOH.
  • Fountain: The Tyler Davidson Fountain may be running. If audible in mics, check omni-pattern wireless capsules first; SM81 and directional mics will largely reject it at normal distances.
Workflow Reminders
  • VCA groups: Drums (Ch 1–10), Bass (Ch 11–12), Guitars (Ch 13–16), Keys/Electronics (Ch 17–20)
  • Anchor channel: Kick In (Ch 1) — set gain structure from the kick, build the mix from the bottom up
  • Soundcheck order: Kick → bass → drums kit → guitars (start with one SM57, then blend Beta27) → acoustic → keys → SPDX → looper
  • SPDX and Looper: Get the drummer to hit every pad before the show so you can hear the sample levels. Comp threshold may need adjustment per pad bank.
  • Gate check: Run the drummer through the full kit solo before band soundcheck — verify all gates open cleanly and release doesn't chop ring on toms and overheads.
  • Dante/wireless status: Confirm vocal channels are routing before show. Channels 25–32 are reserved; verify Dante feed is present and named correctly on the Q225.
