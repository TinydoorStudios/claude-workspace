Show Overview

The Verve Pipe — original 90s alt-rock band at Fifth Third Center Stage, Fountain Square, Cincinnati OH. Dense guitar-forward mix: two guitar positions each running dual-mic rigs (SM57 + Beta 27), two-mic kick (Beta 91 + Beta 52), and four channels of electronics/loops. Vocals arriving via Dante/wireless — not in this patch. Reverb via Seventh Heaven Pro (6 presets below).

PA: L-Acoustics A15 (4× per side, flown) + 8× KS21 subs (delayed arch). System tuned — do not adjust processor settings.
Monitors: Midas M32 (dedicated — does not rotate to FOH at FSQ).
RF: Tempest unit #215217. Dante/wireless vocals on separate route — confirm feed is live before show.
Outdoor note: No natural room — reverb does all the work. Pre-delay is critical for intelligibility. Don't pull decay down as aggressively as Memo.

Reverb — Seventh Heaven Pro (6 Presets)

FSQ is outdoor — no natural room decay. Keep decay at or near factory; the reverb carries all the space. Pre-delay is critical for intelligibility. Early/Late and Late Rolloff at factory unless noted.
① Vocal Hall  |  Halls 1 / Concert Hall  ✓ verified
Preset: Halls 1 / Concert Hall | Decay 2.2s • PreDelay 18ms • VLF −10dB • Early/Late: factory • Late Rolloff: factory • Mix 12%
2.2s factory decay is appropriate for outdoor — the room isn't adding anything here. Primary vocal hall send. VLF at −10dB factory is already conservative; leave it unless the low end gets muddy outdoors.
② Vocal Plate  |  Plates 1 / Vocal Plate  ✓ verified (#06)
Preset: Plates 1 / Vocal Plate | Decay 1.5s • PreDelay 24ms • VLF −19dB • Equal Mix • Late Rolloff: factory • Mix 15%
Factory settings throughout. VLF at −19dB keeps the low end clean. Good workhorse when the hall gets too big outdoors, or as the primary on backgrounds. Pulls back behind the voice without washing it.
③ Vocal — Something Nice  |  Chambers 1 / Sunset Chamber  ✓ verified
Preset: Chambers 1 / Sunset Chamber | Decay 2.15s • PreDelay 20ms • VLF −20dB • Early/Late: factory • Late Rolloff: factory • Mix 10%
Long, warm chamber. At Memo you'd pull decay back — at FSQ let it run at factory 2.15s; the room adds nothing underneath it. The Verve Pipe's catalog suits this quality. Keep mix conservative; it's an enveloping send, not a slapback.
④ Guitar Room  |  Rooms 2 / Guitar Room  ✓ verified (#16)

300ms hardware delay baked in at −14dB — leave it. That's the point of this preset. Feed the SM57 channels (Ch 13, 15); Beta27 blend at lower send level. Mix low; the delay provides depth without wash.

Run it hotter than you would at Memo — outdoors the snare crack has nothing to bounce off. Dense and fast. Watch feedback with open mics; outdoor gain margins are tighter.

Mic Choices & Notes

Kick — Two-Mic Rig

Beta 91 (Ch 1) inside on batter head — attack/click. Beta 52 (Ch 2) for body. Start Ch 1 at unity, bring Ch 2 up for weight. +3 dB low shelf at 60 Hz on Ch 2 feeds the KS21 subs.

Snare

SM57 top and bottom. Polarity check on Ch 4 vs Ch 3 before advancing fader — almost always needs flipping.

Hat / Ride

SM81 on both. Aggressive HPF (450 / 500 Hz). Duckers rather than hard gates on both.

Overheads

SM81 pair, true overhead position. Stereo linked. Check mono sum before advancing.

Bass

Radial ProDI (Ch 11) for fundamental; SM57 on cab (Ch 12) for grit. Polarity check Ch 12 vs Ch 11. DI owns the bottom.

Guitars — Dual-Mic Rigs

Each cab: SM57 (attack, lead) + Beta 27 (warmth, blend). Center = Ch 13/14, Right = Ch 15/16. Start SM57 at unity, Beta 27 6–8 dB below. Polarity check each Beta 27 before blending. Check mono sum for comb filtering around 300–500 Hz.

Acoustic / Keys / Electronics

All passive Radial ProDI. Acoustic EQ assumes onboard piezo — adjust at soundcheck. SPDX: Band 4 flat until you hear the patches. Looper: monitor level as layers build; comp threshold may need live adjustment.
