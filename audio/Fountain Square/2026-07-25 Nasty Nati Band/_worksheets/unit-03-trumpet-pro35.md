             is effectively the lead voice of the group. Brightest and loudest horns in the
             line; transients spike above everything else on stage.
MIC          Audio-Technica PRO 35 — same unit as 02. Switch assumed OUT, though on trumpets it
             is immaterial (nothing musical below 165 Hz). Phantom required, Clip on ch 22/23
             per the sheet's Tall-stand entries — mounts stay as the sheet has them.
SEARCHES     Within-show reuse: this exact instrument × mic unit was researched at Rev 1.0 on
             2026-07-23 for ch 21 (same show, same day's build). Cross-show caching is banned;
             within-show dedupe is allowed, so the Rev 1.0 pass carries. The Unit 02 searches
             this rev re-verified the PRO 35 capsule facts and corrected the switch error.
CAPSULE FACT PRO 35: 50–15,000 Hz, 145 dB max SPL, −18 dB @ 80 Hz via SWITCH; KB adds the voiced
             presence lift and rolled-off extreme top.
             Source: RecordingHacks PRO-35 spec page + KB mic-library.
WEB SAYS     Carried from Rev 1.0: trumpet bark lives 2.5–3 kHz and is the cut that matters live;
             the PRO 35's rolled extreme top means a small lift near 9 kHz buys sizzle back
             without touching the bark.

VERDICT      AGREE — unchanged from Rev 1.0, re-verified against the fresh PRO 35 spec fetch.

             channels must NOT carry identical curves or they comb and pile. Rev 1.0's single
             trumpet keeps its numbers exactly; the second trumpet gets an offset lane.

             needed — Rev 1.0's depths were built for this room and Brian accepted them.
