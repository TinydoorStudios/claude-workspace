# Unit 03 — Snare top × Audix i5  [ch 3, both bands]

INSTRUMENT   DW Collectors Series maple snare. TWO provided: 6.5"×14" and 5.5"×14" (backline).
             Deeper 6.5" reads fatter/lower; 5.5" crisper. Which one is up is a soundcheck fact —
             flagged in notes, not guessed.
EQUIP FACT   Backline also ships a spare head pack (kick batter, snare top and bottom) and TWO
             DW9300 snare stands — i.e. both snares can be up at once, so a swap between bands is
             physically set up for. Relevant to the changeover sheet.
MIC          Audix i5, cardioid dynamic. Not a ribbon, not TOUR.
SEARCHES     "Audix i5 frequency response presence peak dB snare" (RecordingHacks profile)
             + KB cross-read of the snare row in eq-starting-points
CAPSULE FACT RecordingHacks: 50 Hz–16 kHz (−3 dB), "a +5dB peak at 150 Hz and a pronounced +9dB
             peak at 5,500 Hz", cardioid, 140 dB max SPL.
WEB SAYS     More open and more body than an SM57; the crack is voiced in; that 5.5 k peak can get
             harsh on a cracky snare.
KB SAYS      mic-library: "Body lift ~+5dB@150Hz, presence ~+9dB@5.5kHz, mids slightly scooped vs
             SM57 — more open, more body." → ease off body, crack, snap; tame harsh ~5500 Hz.
VERDICT      AGREE — identical numbers on both sides, KB row confirmed by the external profile.
LOCKER       First call ("Audix i5 — Snare, tight cardioid, punchy"). No fork.
GENRE BEND   BLSH: backbeat inside a dense, reverby psych mix — it has to cut without turning
             brittle; the +5 @ 150 body stays, it's what makes the groove feel fat.
             JRW: the snare IS the signature of a Zepp-meets-'50s show — huge, fat, cracking. Keep
             MORE wood than BLSH (box cut −7 not −8) and trim the 5.5 k peak only −3, so the
             capsule's crack survives.
VENUE BEND   FSQ: box/shell cut deep (−7/−8, tight Q). RH 92 % → the 5.5 k peak arrives fully
             through the A15s, so it gets trimmed on both bands rather than left alone.
DRAFT BANDS  BLSH: HPF 100 · LPF OFF · B4 −4 @ 5500 Q2.5 · B3 −8 @ 500 Q1.8 · B2 FLAT · B1 FLAT
             JRW:  HPF  90 · LPF OFF · B4 −3 @ 5500 Q2.5 · B3 −7 @ 500 Q1.8 · B2 FLAT · B1 FLAT
GATE CHECK   No boosts. The crack people reach for at 5 kHz is the capsule's own +9 dB — the desk's
             move there is −3/−4. The +5 @ 150 body lift is left untouched rather than "restored"
             with a low bell, which would double it.
