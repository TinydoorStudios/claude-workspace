# Unit 04 — Snare bottom (wires) × Sennheiser e604  [ch 4, both bands]

INSTRUMENT   Underside of the DW Collectors maple snare. Wires only — this channel exists to add
             rattle and articulation under the i5, never body.
EQUIP FACT   Backline spare-head pack includes a snare BOTTOM head, so the resonant head is a fresh
             thin snare-side — bright, lively wires, more HF than a worn head. Reinforces the
             no-boost call up top.
MIC          Sennheiser e604 clip-on dynamic. Not a ribbon, not TOUR.
SEARCHES     "Sennheiser e604 frequency response chart presence peak dB tom mic low end rolloff"
             → RecordingHacks e604 profile (fetched)
CAPSULE FACT RecordingHacks: published 40–18,000 Hz; at 5 cm the proximity effect is roughly
             "+9dB @ 85Hz"; "High mids are emphasized, +3dB from 3–5kHz"; "flat high end through
             15kHz and a rapid dropoff in response above ~16kHz"; wide-cardioid below 2 kHz turning
             supercardioid above 2 kHz, 25 dB rejection past 60° off-axis at 16 kHz.
WEB SAYS     Fast, convenient clip mic; the proximity lift is severe at clip distance; the 3–5 kHz
             emphasis is what gives it its attack.
KB SAYS      mic-library: "Tom/snare clip dynamic, scooped low-mids, voiced attack. Convenient,
             fast. Weakness: thinner/boxier than a D-series, modest low end." → ease off attack, mud.
VERDICT      AGREE — "voiced attack" is the measured +3 dB at 3–5 kHz; "modest low end" is the
             40 Hz limit. The web adds the +9 dB @ 85 Hz proximity figure the KB doesn't carry.
LOCKER       First call for a clip tom/snare mic. No fork.
GENRE BEND   BLSH: wires ride under the groove — clean and tight. JRW: a fatter '50s/Zepp snare
             wants a touch more of the drum in the bottom mic, so the HPF sits 20 Hz lower and the
             box cut is one step shallower.
VENUE BEND   FSQ: the box cut at 400 goes to −7/−6. HPF set HIGH (200–220) because outdoors the
             only thing below that on this capsule is kick bleed plus the +9 dB proximity boom.
DRAFT BANDS  BLSH: HPF 220 · LPF OFF · B4 FLAT · B3 −7 @ 400 Q2.0 · B2 FLAT · B1 FLAT
             JRW:  HPF 200 · LPF OFF · B4 FLAT · B3 −6 @ 400 Q2.0 · B2 FLAT · B1 FLAT
GATE CHECK   No boosts. The reflex move on a bottom snare mic is a lift at 4–8 kHz for wire detail —
             but the capsule already emphasises 3–5 kHz by +3 dB and is flat to 15 kHz, so the
             rattle is delivered without help. At 92 % RH it also arrives intact at the back of the
             plaza. B4 stays FLAT rather than boosted.
             POLARITY: bottom mic is flipped against the top — set at soundcheck, in notes.
QUESTIONS    none
