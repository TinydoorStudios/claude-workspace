# Unit 08 — Overheads × Shure Beta 27 (matched pair)  [fader 9 — STEREO, both bands]

INSTRUMENT   Drum overheads on DW9000 boom stands (six supplied). Cymbals are BAND-SUPPLIED — the
             quote says "ARTIST RIDER DOES NOT REQUEST CYMBALS" — so cymbal weight/pitch is unknown
             and differs between the two bands even though the shells don't.
EQUIP FACT   Two bands sharing one kit but bringing their own cymbals means the overhead channel is
             the ONE drum channel whose source genuinely changes at the break. That is why the two
             bands' overhead curves differ more than their kick curves do, and why it is called out
             on the changeover sheet.
MIC          Shure Beta 27, supercardioid large-diaphragm condenser, pair. Not a ribbon, not TOUR.
             Switches: HPF assumed OUT (flat) and the −15 dB pad assumed OUT — fallback in mic_notes.
             *** TEMPLATE RULE: fader 9 "Overheads" is a STEREO channel — BOTH mics live on that one
             fader. Fader 10 is SNARE PL8, the snare plate return, never an input. ***
SEARCHES     "Shure Beta 27 frequency response peaks pad polar pattern" → RecordingHacks Beta 27 (fetched)
CAPSULE FACT RecordingHacks: 20–20,000 Hz, nominally flat "from ~60Hz to 3kHz"; two HF peaks of
             "+2dB or less" at 5500 Hz and 9 kHz; HF −3 dB point beyond 15 kHz; switchable HPF
             "-6dB/octave @ 115Hz" or "-18dB/octave @ 80Hz"; "-15dB" pad; supercardioid "extremely
             consistent below 6400Hz" with deep, consistent nulls at 120°.
WEB SAYS     An open, clean, honest instrument condenser — flatter than an SM57, mild HF lift only.
KB SAYS      mic-library: "Supercardioid LDC, flat 60Hz-3kHz, small peaks +2dB at 5.5k and 9k, -15dB
             pad, flatter than SM57. Open, clean cab/instrument mic. Weakness: slight 9k fizz on
             bright amps." → ease off box, honk; tame harsh ~9000 Hz.
VERDICT      AGREE — the KB row and the external profile give identical numbers, including both peaks.
LOCKER       mic-library lists Beta 27 for "Vocals, overheads, acoustic instruments" — this is a
             first-call use. The other overhead-capable pair Brian owns is the SM81, and both SM81s
             are already committed on this show (hat ch 5, aux perc ch 23 for BLSH), so no free
             alternative exists. No fork.
GENRE BEND   BLSH: psych/groove cymbal colour matters — but see the venue bend, it still gets trimmed.
             JRW: Zepp/'50s cymbals are darker and washier and sit further back; deeper top trim.
VENUE BEND   *** WIND: gusts 17–19 mph at downbeat (Open-Meteo, fetched). The overhead pair is the
             show's most wind-exposed input → HPF 300. (Reference point: 22–28 mph put a previous
             FSQ build at 400; this is the lighter case, so 300 not 400.) ***
             RH 92 %: both baked peaks arrive intact, so 9 k is cut on both bands.
DRAFT BANDS  BLSH: HPF 300 · LPF OFF · B4 −3 @ 9000 Q2.0 · B3 −7 @ 500 Q1.8 · B2 FLAT · B1 FLAT
             JRW:  HPF 300 · LPF OFF · B4 −4 @ 9000 Q2.0 · B3 −6 @ 500 Q1.8 · B2 FLAT · B1 FLAT
GATE CHECK   No boosts. The +2 dB peaks at 5.5 k and 9 k are small but real, and at 92 % RH there is
             no air loss to excuse reinforcing them — the 9 k band is a cut. B3 clears the shell and
             stand wash the supercardioid pattern still lets through; it is held at −7/−6 rather
             than −9 because the capsule is FLAT through there and there is no scoop to lean on,
             so an over-deep cut would hollow the kit out.
QUESTIONS    none
