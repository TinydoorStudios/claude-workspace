GENRE BEND   BLSH — the EPK sells "lush layers of guitar and synth" and reviewers call the record
             "shimmering retro soul". Those pads sit exactly in the mud zone, so they get cleared —
             but only to −6, not the full FSQ −8/−9, because retro-soul warmth is a feature of the
             sound rather than a defect (the same reconciliation Brian accepted on the Repertoire
             build: outdoors the mud the deep-cut rule exists to fight is MECHANICAL — box, bleed,
             cab resonance — and an open plaza contributes none of its own).
             JRW — this is the barrelhouse piano voice. The style is documented as percussive and
             hard-struck, with "a pounding, train-like beat (walking figures, repeated basses, and
             chordal stomps)" in the left hand and many players using no pedal at all "to keep the
             attack crisp and loud" (American Blues Scene / Wikipedia). The left hand IS the engine,
             so the low-mids do NOT get scooped the way a pad keyboard would be — HPF stays low at
             50 Hz and the 350 cut is held to −6.
VENUE BEND   FSQ: mud cleared, but capped at −6 on every keys channel for the reason above.
