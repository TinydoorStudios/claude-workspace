# Unit 09 — Bass DI × Whirlwind IMP 2  [ch 11, both bands]

INSTRUMENT   Electric bass. BLSH = Jackie O'Brien (syncopated, tension-building lines — EARMILK on
             "Lifers Only"); JRW = Zepp-lineage rock bass, big and round.
EQUIP FACT   Backline bass rig: Ampeg SVT-CL head into an Ampeg SVT-410HLF plus an Ampeg SVT 15e.
             The 410HLF is front/rear-ported and, per Ampeg/retail spec, reaches "down to 28Hz"
             where "most 4x10 cabinets bottom out around 40-50Hz", with a 1" compression driver
             carrying highs "up to 4kHz" behind an L-Pad. So the RIG has enormous low reach — but
             the DI is upstream of it, which is exactly why the DI carries the bottom to the PA and
             the cab mic does not.
MIC          Whirlwind IMP 2 passive DI. *** DI → LOCKER FORK EXEMPT (Brian's rule, 2026-07-26). ***
SEARCHES     "Whirlwind IMP 2 passive DI transformer frequency response spec high frequency loss keyboard bass"
             "SM57 on bass cabinet live sound thin low end blend with DI forum" (shared with unit 10)
CAPSULE FACT Whirlwind/Sweetwater/zZounds: frequency response "20Hz-20kHz ±1dB", −20 dB signal loss,
             133:1 impedance ratio, 20 kΩ in / 150 Ω out, TRHL transformer. Flat within ±1 dB —
             there is no voicing here to work around.
WEB SAYS     Neutral workhorse for guitars, basses and keyboards; passive, so it is honest rather
             than flattering and gives up a little level.
KB SAYS      mic-library: "Passive DI, neutral/honest, reliable. Bass/keys direct. Weakness: passive
             = slight HF/level loss into low-Z, no active sparkle." → apply template as-is.
VERDICT      AGREE — ±1 dB flat on both sides. Nothing baked, so every move here is mine to justify.
LOCKER       Exempt (DI).
GENRE BEND   BLSH: psych-groove basslines carry the dance floor and need note DEFINITION as well as
             weight — the syncopation is unreadable if only the fundamental gets through.
             JRW: Zepp bass is rounder and heavier with less finger detail; more weight, less
             definition boost.
VENUE BEND   FSQ: no room gain, the KS21 arch is the entire bottom end. Mud cut deep at 300. Low
             bell deliberately placed at 50 Hz — NOT 70–80 — so it does not collide with the 24"
             kick's ~75 Hz fundamental. That separation is a venue/arrangement decision, not a
             mic one.
DRAFT BANDS  BLSH: HPF 35 · LPF OFF · B4 FLAT · B3 +3 @ 800 Q1.4 · B2 −7 @ 300 Q2.0 · B1 +3 @ 50 Q1.2
             JRW:  HPF 35 · LPF OFF · B4 FLAT · B3 +2 @ 800 Q1.4 · B2 −6 @ 300 Q2.0 · B1 +4 @ 50 Q1.2
GATE CHECK   Both boosts pass, and the reason is the same in each case: the IMP 2 is flat to ±1 dB
             across the whole band, so there is no baked peak anywhere for a boost to stack on.
             This is the one channel type where the gate is satisfied by measured flatness rather
             than by finding a hole in a curve.
             The +3/+2 @ 800 is inside the 700 Hz–2 kHz definition region and is NOT an HF move, so
             the 92 % RH no-HF-boost rule does not reach it — that rule governs above ~4 kHz, where
             air absorption actually lives. B4 stays FLAT accordingly.
             Two-source lane split with ch 12: the DI OWNS EVERYTHING BELOW 150 Hz; the SM57 is
             high-passed at 150/140 so it cannot stack there.
QUESTIONS    Is the DI taken pre-amp (instrument) or off the SVT-CL's balanced out? Post-head the
             tone is already SVT-voiced and the 300 Hz cut can come back a step. Soundcheck item,
             written into notes.
