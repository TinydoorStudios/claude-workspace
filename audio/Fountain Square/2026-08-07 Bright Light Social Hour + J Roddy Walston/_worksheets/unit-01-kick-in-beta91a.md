# Unit 01 — Kick (attack layer) × Shure Beta 91A  [ch 1, both bands]

INSTRUMENT   DW Collectors Series maple kick, 18"D × 24"W (backline quote 26-0645). Boundary mic
             lying inside on the shell floor. Attack/click layer of a two-mic kick.
EQUIP FACT   A 24" kick's resonant fundamental sits low-70s to mid-80s Hz (vs 80–90 Hz for a 22")
             and "most kick drum attack" lands ~4 kHz — DrumChat / Gearspace / drum-tuning.com.
             So the body lives at ~75–80 Hz on THIS drum, not the reflex 60 Hz.
MIC          Shure Beta 91A, half-cardioid boundary condenser. Contour switch ASSUMED FLAT —
             fallback written into mic_notes. Not a ribbon, not TOUR (3CDC backline).
SEARCHES     "Shure Beta 91A frequency response curve boundary kick mic contour switch 400Hz dB spec"
             "24 inch kick drum fundamental frequency vs 22 inch DW Collectors maple tuning low end live sound"
CAPSULE FACT Shure/RecordingHacks/Mix: the contour switch "cuts 7dB at 400Hz"; the flat setting has
             "an emphasis from 4kHz to about 9kHz [which] ensures a distinct attack sound."
WEB SAYS     Flat position = natural, contour = low-mid scoop for punch + attack. Boundary mounting
             picks up shell boxiness 300–500 Hz. Engineers commonly leave it flat and cut on the desk.
KB SAYS      mic-library: "Half-cardioid boundary kick/piano mic. Solid boundary low end +
             pronounced beater click; nominally flat (contour switch cuts 7dB@400 if engaged).
             Weakness: picks up shell boxiness ~300-500." → ease off boom; tame box ~400 Hz.
VERDICT      AGREE — the 7 dB @ 400 figure and the 4–9 kHz attack emphasis match exactly.
LOCKER       First call. mic-library: "Shure Beta 91 — Kick, attack layer inside on head; pair with
             Beta 52." That is literally this pairing. No fork.
GENRE BEND   BLSH (psych/groove, danceable): kick is a felt groove anchor — definition enough to
             read on a dance floor, not a metal click.
             JRW (Zepp meets '50s rock — Brian): bigger and rounder, beater present but not clicky;
             '50s rock kick is felt, not heard. Trim the baked attack one step further.
VENUE BEND   FSQ open air, no room gain → box cut runs to −8 (deep-cut rule). RH 82→94 % means the
             4–9 kHz emphasis arrives INTACT at the back of the plaza → trim it, never add to it.
DRAFT BANDS  BLSH: HPF 60 · LPF OFF · B4 −3 @ 6300 Q1.6 · B3 −8 @ 400 Q1.8 · B2 −5 @ 200 Q1.8 · B1 FLAT
             JRW:  HPF 50 · LPF OFF · B4 −4 @ 6300 Q1.6 · B3 −8 @ 400 Q1.8 · B2 −4 @ 200 Q1.8 · B1 FLAT
GATE CHECK   No boosts on this channel — nothing to justify. The 4–9 kHz attack is BAKED, so the
             desk's job there is −3/−4, not +. Two-mic lane split: 91A owns the ATTACK band
             (4–9 kHz, capsule-supplied, lightly tamed); Beta 52A owns BODY. Neither mic is boosted
             in the other's lane, top or bottom — the 91A's 200 Hz is cut so the 52A owns the weight.
             Polarity: sum both in mono at soundcheck, flip the 52A if thinner.
