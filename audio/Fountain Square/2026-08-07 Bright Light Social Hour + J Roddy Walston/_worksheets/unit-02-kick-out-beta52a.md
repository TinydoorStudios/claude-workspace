# Unit 02 — Kick (body layer) × Shure Beta 52A  [ch 2, both bands]

INSTRUMENT   Same DW Collectors 18"×24" maple kick, mic at the port. Body/thump core.
EQUIP FACT   As unit 01 — 24" shell, fundamental low-70s/mid-80s Hz. FSQ KS21 arch carries below
             that, so the channel's job is placement, not sub manufacture.
MIC          Shure Beta 52A, supercardioid dynamic, 174 dB SPL. Not a ribbon, not TOUR.
SEARCHES     "Shure Beta 52A frequency response 4kHz presence peak low frequency spec kick drum EQ live"
             (+ the 24" kick tuning search shared with unit 01)
CAPSULE FACT Shure spec / DrumHelper / FrontEndAudio: 20 Hz–10 kHz tailored response with "a low-Q,
             high-end boost of about +7dB, reaching its peak around 4kHz", targeting the beater
             strike; supercardioid gives a pronounced proximity low-end lift.
WEB SAYS     Purpose-voiced kick mic — big lows and a beater peak baked in; needs very little EQ.
KB SAYS      mic-library: "Tailored kick dynamic, scooped low-mids, presence lift ~4kHz, big lows.
             Click+thump voiced in." → ease off attack, box, mud.
VERDICT      AGREE — +7 dB @ 4 kHz and the scooped low-mids line up on both sides.
LOCKER       First call ("Shure Beta 52 — Kick, body and thump layer (inside)"). No fork.
GENRE BEND   BLSH: tight, danceable thump. JRW: rounder and bigger, one step less beater.
VENUE BEND   FSQ deep-cut rule applies to the box at 350 — but only to −7/−6, NOT −9, because the
             capsule has already scooped the low-mids and double-cutting a scoop is the capsule
             gate running in reverse.
DRAFT BANDS  BLSH: HPF 30 · LPF OFF · B4 −4 @ 4000 Q1.6 · B3 −7 @ 350 Q2.0 · B2 FLAT · B1 FLAT
             JRW:  HPF 30 · LPF OFF · B4 −5 @ 4000 Q1.6 · B3 −6 @ 350 Q2.0 · B2 FLAT · B1 FLAT
GATE CHECK   No boosts. Deliberately NO low bell: the 52A's big low end is baked in and the drum's
             ~75 Hz fundamental sits inside it — a +3 there would be a boost stacked on a voiced
             region, exactly what the gate forbids. The weight comes from the capsule plus the
             KS21s; the desk only clears the mud in front of it.
             Two-mic lane split (the de-stack): BOTH mics carry an attack emphasis — 91A at 4–9 kHz,
             52A at +7 dB @ 4 kHz. Stacking them is the classic failure, so the 52A's 4 kHz is
             trimmed −4/−5 and it becomes a pure body mic; the 91A keeps the attack lane.
             Bottom lane: 52A owns everything below 200; the 91A is cut there.
QUESTIONS    none
