# Unit 03 — Hi-hat × Shure SM81
INSTRUMENT   Rock hats; sits ~6" from the snare, so snare bleed is the enemy.
MIC          Shure SM81 SDC cardioid, 48V. Switches: LF flat / −6 dB/oct <100 Hz / −18 dB/oct <80 Hz; −10 dB pad.
             ASSUMED: LF switch FLAT, pad OFF (desk HPF does the work). Fallback: if the switch is on the 18 dB step, drop desk HPF to 200.
SEARCHES     "Shure SM81 hi-hat frequency response flat rolloff switch live EQ"; "site:forums.prosoundweb.com SM81 hi hat EQ HPF"
CAPSULE FACT Shure SM81 user guide: flat 20 Hz–20 kHz; LF rolloff options −6 dB/oct below 100 Hz or −18 dB/oct below 80 Hz; −10 dB pad.
WEB SAYS     PSW: standard hat mic; HPF hard, cut lows; optional +few dB ~7k shimmer.
KB SAYS      mic-library: "ruler-flat SM81 takes the template straight". eq-starting-points hat row cross-checked.
