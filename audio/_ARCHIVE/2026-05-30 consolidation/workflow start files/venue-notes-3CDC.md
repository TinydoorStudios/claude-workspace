## General Outdoor Notes (All 3CDC Venues)

- All venues are outdoors — no room acoustics to fight, but wind and ambient noise raise the noise floor
- Gain-before-feedback margins are tighter than indoors; EQ should favor cuts over boosts on all vocal channels (same rule as Memo applies here)
- Weather affects everything: wind on ribbon mics is a non-starter, humidity affects wireless RF
- RF coordination: Tempest units are venue-specific (see individual venue above) — do not swap units between venues without rechecking frequencies
- No system processor adjustments at any venue unless Brian explicitly authorizes it
