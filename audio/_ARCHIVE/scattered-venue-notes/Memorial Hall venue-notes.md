# Memorial Hall — Venue Notes

Cincinnati, OH | Jazz At The Memo

---

## Room

- 556 seats
- Stage: 37'4" W × 22'3" D, hardwood
- Beaux Arts, built 1908
- Piano storage: stage right
- Working RT60: ~1.6s (revised — 2.2s was pre-renovation/empty estimate; ~1.6s is the working figure)

## Problem Frequencies

Always treat in EQ, especially on crowd/ambient mics:

- 63 Hz
- 125 Hz
- 200 Hz
- 250–315 Hz (standing waves)

## Console

DiGiCo Quantum 225

## PA System

**Hangs:** 4 total — 2 main floor, 2 balcony
- Soundbridge 7108XY, 3 per hang (12 cabs total)

**Subs:** 2× Soundbridge 7218SWX (dual 18"), stacked in stage corners

**Front Fills:** 4× Soundbridge MTM cabs across stage front

**Processing:** Lake (main PA) | BSS (subs)

*PA is tuned — do not adjust system processor settings.*

## Royer AxeMount (SR Guitar)

CH13 SM57 + CH15 R-121

- SM57 = primary. Set level first.
- R-121 = blend. Bring up from zero until brittleness reduces.
- Typical blend: R-121 sits 6–10dB below SM57
- Polarity check in mono — should be fuller than either alone; flip R-121 if thinner
- ⚠ NO 48V on R-121 under any circumstances
- Group both to same VCA for level riding
- Post-blend: check 300–500Hz buildup — notch −2 to −3dB on bus EQ if needed
