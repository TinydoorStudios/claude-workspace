# Live Sound KB — System Config

## Purpose

Working reference for Brian Lloyd's live sound and recording practice. 20+ years experience; heavy classical work. Covers venues, consoles, mic library, EQ approaches, show workflows, and multitrack/post.

## Focus Areas

1. Venue-specific signal chains, patching, and acoustic notes
2. Console workflow and recall notes (DiGiCo, Wing, CL3, M32)
3. Mic library — kit choices, placement, classical-specific technique
4. EQ starting points by instrument and venue
5. Show document workflow (show folders, input lists, stage plots, master PDFs)
6. Multitrack recording workflow (Studio One 7)
7. Post and mastering workflow (WaveLab 12)
8. Active projects

## Writing Rules

- Clear and direct. Warm but not corporate.
- Prose paragraphs over bullet soup.
- Technical shorthand is fine — this is an expert KB.
- Venue shorthand always use the canonical abbreviation first mention per article: Memo (Memorial Hall), FSQ (Fountain Square), ESP (Elm Street Plaza), WP (Washington Park), CSP (Court Street Plaza), ZP (Zeigler Park), IA (Imagination Alley).
- No banned words: "straightforward", "genuinely", "honestly".
- US English spelling.
