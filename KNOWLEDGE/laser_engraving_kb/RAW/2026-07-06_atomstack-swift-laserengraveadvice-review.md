## Basic Product Information (verbatim)

- Product Name: ATOMSTACK Swift 12W Laser Engraver
- Laser Power: 12W Optical Power (60000 mW Machine Power)
- Engraving Area: 300 x 300 mm
- Engraving Speed: Up to 400 mm/s
- Spot Precision: 0.06 x 0.08 mm
- Connectivity: Type-C, USB, ATOMSTACK App, Lightburn, LaserGRBL

Note: this source lists spot size as 0.06 x 0.08mm, while the Hoffman Engineering review (same knowledge base, separate RAW file) lists 0.08 x 0.04mm "compressed." Both cite the same underlying spec sheet language but round/report it differently — flagged in Wiki/QUESTIONS.md rather than reconciled.

## Pros and Cons (verbatim)

**Pros:**
- Impressive Power and Speed: true 12W optical power + 400mm/s speed
- High Precision Engraving: compressed 0.06mm laser spot
- User-Friendly for All Levels: straightforward assembly + intuitive mobile app; LightBurn for advanced users
- Solid Build Quality: all-metal construction

**Cons:**
- Advanced Software Learning Curve: mastering LightBurn takes time
- Workspace Limitation: 300x300mm restricts very large pieces
- Requires Safety Diligence: Class 4 laser — proper ventilation and eye protection essential

## Materials confirmed for this machine (verbatim)

"It can engrave a wide variety of materials including wood, metal, dark acrylic, glass, leather, bamboo, stainless steel, and more. For cutting, it can handle materials like wood (up to 15mm), acrylic (up to 12mm), and cardboard."
