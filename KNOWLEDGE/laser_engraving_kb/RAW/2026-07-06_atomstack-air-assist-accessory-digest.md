Search context: web search + product page fetch confirming what air assist accessory exists for the Swift specifically, since Brian's machine does not have one installed.

## Findings

The Swift ships with an air assist port on the module but no pump (confirmed independently in the Hoffman Engineering review, separate RAW file). AtomStack sells a dedicated add-on for this machine: the **AtomStack F40 Air Assist Set for Swift Laser Engraver**, listed around $59 direct from atomstack.com (product collection: atomstack.com/collections/swift-air-assist). AtomStack's own product copy describes air-assisted accessories generally as reducing surface temperature of the workpiece quickly, preventing excessive burning, and producing cleaner/smoother cut edges. Hoffman Engineering's review separately lists a "12W Air Assist Bundle" (machine + air assist together) at $239 as a purchase option.

No third-party (non-AtomStack) air assist compatibility was confirmed in this search — the F40 set is the manufacturer-matched option for the Swift's port.
