                                                         DMI-DANTE 64@96



KEY FEATURES
The DiGiCo DMI-DANTE 64@96 is an option card to
expand the I/O of selected DiGiCo products. The DMI-
DANTE 64@96 provides 64 inputs and 64 outputs at both
48kHz and 96kHz.


■      64 inputs and 64 outputs at 48kHz and 96kHz
■      1 RJ45 port
■      2 Ethercon ports
■      Two port modes - switched and redundant
■      Inbuilt SRC



CONNECTIONS

    1 x RJ45 port
    2 x Ethercon ports




SIGNAL PROCESSING

    64 Inputs and 64 Outputs at 48kHz and 96kHz
                                                         DiGiCo Multichannel Interface
    Inbuilt SRC between 48kHz and 96kHz

                                                         DiGiCo DMI cards are perfect for expanding your
                                                         console I/O, providing interfaces with industry formats
                                                         be it Analogue expansion, MADI, Dante, Waves
OPTIONS                                                  Soundgrid or Calrec’s Hydra 2 Network.

    Compatible With: S-Series / SD12 / Quantum Range /   All bases are covered
    Orange Box / 4REA4
