# 2.6 DMI - ME - A3232 Cards

*DMI Cards — manual page 51*

![DMI-ME card rear panel: single port for Allen & Heath ME Personal Mixing System](/figures/gs-p051-1.png)

![DMI-A3232 card rear panel: two Cat5 ports (1 and 2) with Activity/Error indicators, for A168 STAGE Racks](/figures/gs-p051-2.png)

The DMI-ME provides 40 outputs at 48kHz and 96kHz to Allen & Heath ME-1 or ME-500 Personal Mixers. There are no controls on the card.

The DMI-A3232 (NOT COMPATIBLE WITH Q2) allows connection to A168 STAGE Racks. Each Cat5 port supports up to 32 I/O, for a total channel count of 64 I/O. The rack can be run in two different modes. In Cascade Mode, up to two A168 racks can be connected to each port. In redundant mode, one A168 rack can be connected with redundant cabling to both ports on the card. When a DMI-A3232 is connected, each rack connected will be shown in a different view. The mode is changed by touching the desired mode in the Port Control Mode section. QUANTUM consoles have control over gain, phantom power and pads of the rack.

NOTE: When changing the Port Control Mode, there is a physical switch on the A168 STAGE racks that must also be changed.

NOTE: The primary and secondary ports on the racks are not interchangeable. The primary port must be used as the main connection.
