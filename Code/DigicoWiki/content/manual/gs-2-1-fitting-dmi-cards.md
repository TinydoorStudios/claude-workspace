# 2.1 Fitting DMI Cards

*DMI Cards — manual pages 40–41*

NOTE: DMI cards are NOT hot swappable and therefore they should NEVER be fitted or removed from the console while the console is powered on.

To fit a DMI card:

1. Remove the four screws from the console blanking plate — this will expose a hole in the rear panel with an internal connector and a white slider on each side of the hole. Keep the four screws safe in order to secure the card in place.

![DMI Internal Socket (left) and card fitting into the slider grooves on the rear panel (right)](/figures/gs-p040-2.png)

NOTE: Before fitting the DMI card, carefully examine the internal connector, as in some cases this may have a protective cover that must be removed before fitting the card. If the cover is present, remove it before proceeding.

NOTE: With the DMI-MADI C there are 2 internal switches that must be set to the A position for use in the console. Check these switch positions (as pictured below) before fitting the card.

![DMI-MADI C internal switches set to Position A for console use](/figures/gs-p041-1.png)

2. Carefully slide the DMI card into the hole using the white slider grooves as a guide, and ensure it pushes into the internal connector correctly with a click.
3. Replace the 4 fitting screws.
