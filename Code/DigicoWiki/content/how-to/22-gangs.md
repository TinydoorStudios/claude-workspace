# Gang channels together

A gang links every control on a set of channels (faders, EQ, dynamics, sends…) so a move on one happens on all. Pan and polarity are excluded. Unlike a CG there's no master; any member drives the rest. Fader moves are relative in dB.

1. Press **LCD Function** on the section, then **GANG**. To gang across both sections, activate GANG on each first.
2. Press the select button of every channel to link. The GANG symbol on each strip takes the gang's colour.
3. Press GANG again to close that gang and start a new one.

To take a channel out, press its select button while in GANG mode. Gangs can't otherwise be edited once made.

Hold **Option/All** to temporarily isolate the assigned channel from its gang for one adjustment. Holding it while adjusting also applies the change to every channel in the bank.

Options > **Gangs** sets which controls each channel type shares when ganged.

Typical uses: a stereo pair of mics that must move together, or eight drum mics you want to trim as one during soundcheck.

Manual: [Reference 1.8.3 Gang](/reference/1-8-lcd-functions), [2.5.4 Options > Gangs](/reference/2-5-options).
