# Multi channels (stereo pairs, LCR, 5.1 as one strip)

A Multi folds several mono channels into one strip with a master fader and mute, keeping the components available when you unfold it. Use it for a drum overhead pair you want on one fader, or any LCR/5.1 source.

1. Route each component through its own mono channel as normal.
2. Open Channel Setup on the first component and press **Multi**, or press LCD Function > **CREATE MULTI** on an *unassigned* fader slot.
3. In Setup Multi Channels choose the format (LCR, LCRS, 5.1, or **multi-input** for up to 11 components), then press the select buttons of the other components in order. Choose whether the originals stay on the surface (*leave*).

![Create a Multi](/figures/ref-p063-1.png)

**Unfold** on the strip shows every component with a master to their left; **FOLD** collapses it. In the unfolded master, **Set Links** links component controls (like a gang, limited to the multi), and the trim/filters/eq/dynamics/faders buttons copy the folded channel's settings to all components. **Folded Control** chooses which component's input, EQ, dynamics or aux sends show in the folded strip.

The multi's fader and mute always act on all members. A flashing mute means the master is muted but a member has been opened individually. **Edit Multi** changes membership or deletes the multi.

Multi-channel busses (LCR, 5.1) are created in Session Structure.
