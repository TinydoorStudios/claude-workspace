# Solo, PFL/AFL and headphones

![Solos — Quantum 2](/figures/q2-solos.png)

There are two solo busses. Every channel can be assigned to solo 1, solo 2 or both, so one bus can feed the headphones and the other a wedge or a cue send.

## The Solo panel

Master screen > **Solos**. The left half is Solo 1, the right half Solo 2; they work identically.

![Solo panel](/figures/ref-p143-1.png)

Top row (also on the surface):

- **pfl / afl** toggles the pick-off point. AFL is post-fader.
- **multi / single**: multi lets several channels stack on the bus; single replaces the previous solo when you press another.
- **Clear** drops every solo on that bus. The surface button is ringed blue when anything is soloed.
- Solo **trim** (±18 dB) is always on the surface encoder.

Middle: **auto solo** list (channels that join every solo, e.g. FX returns; enable with the *disabled/enabled* button under the list; only works in multi mode), solo **delay** (0 to 2.7 s), bus format **mono/stereo/surround**.

## Headphones

Press **headphones** (bottom left of each solo display; blue when active) to send that bus to the headphone socket. Headphone level is the output pot on screen and the headphone encoder on the surface; the mute next to it kills the phones (ringed red when muted). With nothing soloed you hear the **no solo** source, which is the master bus by default. Change it with the **no solo** button: pick any group, aux or matrix.

## Solo to a wedge or a cue output

Press **direct outs** in the solo output area and route the bus to a socket like any output. The **insert** button gives the solo bus an insert (only on its direct output, not the headphones). *assign level to master* puts the solo output level on a master fader (LCD goes green, fader LCD becomes a solo CLEAR button).

## Which bus does a channel use

Channel Setup (top of the strip), bottom section: **solo 1** / **solo 2** buttons, plus **auto solo**. Or press LCD Function > **SOLO CHOICE** and use the LCD buttons to cycle 1 / 2 / 1+2 per channel.

## True Solo

Quantum only: Solos panel > **True Solo** copies the processing on an output bus onto the solo bus, so what you hear soloing a monitor mix includes that mix's EQ and dynamics, updated live.

## Options that change solo behaviour

Options > **Solo**: solo assigns channel to surface (default on), solo displays insert/FX panels, solo assigns aux sends to faders (default on, that's how "aux to faders" happens when you solo an aux), line check solo bus, restrict PFL/AFL choice to inputs.
