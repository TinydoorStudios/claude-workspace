# DiGiCo Quantum 225 Wiki

Staff reference for the Quantum 225 at Memorial Hall and Fountain Square, the DMI cards in it (Dante 64@96, KLANG, Waves, MADI) and the stage I/O it talks to (DQ-Rack, MQ-Rack, A168D Stage). Type what you're trying to do into the search box at the top: every page is indexed, including the full DiGiCo manuals.

Console software: **V22, build v2242 (June 2026)**. The reference manual mirrored here is Issue H (written for V20 and later); everything V22 added is in the [V22 release notes](/docs/v22-release-notes). Screenshots marked *Quantum 2 offline software, V22* are the current UI; figures marked *manual* are DiGiCo's own and can show an SD-series or older layout.
