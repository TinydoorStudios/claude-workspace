# Fitting or swapping a DMI card

DMI cards are **not hot-swappable**. Console powered off, both PSUs.

1. Shut down properly (System > Shutdown), switch off both PSUs.
2. Remove the four screws holding the blanking plate on the slot. Keep them.
3. Look inside: some slots ship with a protective cover on the internal connector; remove it. On a **DMI-MADI C** set the two internal switches to **A** first.
4. Slide the card in on the white guide rails until it clicks into the connector. Fit the four screws.
5. Power up. Setup > Audio I/O: the card appears as a port; **conform** it (or Conform All Ports).

![DMI slot with blanking plate removed](/figures/ref-p224-2.png)

KLANG goes in slot 2 by KLANG's recommendation. The Dante card's Control port and the KLANG control ports both want to be on the console network; plan the cables before the card goes in.
