We're required by the city to maintain a strict 95 dB limit, measured at the FOH position. All engineers (house or talent) are held to that level.
Failure to comply with the below may result in a warning or termination of the show, with payment canceled or delayed. Please read and acknowledge:
  • Content: Family-friendly only — no foul language or gestures. Applies to prerecorded tracks, live vocals, and sound check.
  • Sound limit: Strict 95 dB at FOH for all engineers, house or talent.
  • Performer safety: Stay on the stage. No crowd surfing, climbing, jumping off stage, or stepping on sound equipment.
  • Audience safety: Do not throw or shoot anything into the crowd (confetti, t-shirts, bottles, merch/CDs, etc.).
  • Weather: Rain or shine. Booking evaluates weather about 3 hours before start. If you don't hear otherwise, assume the show goes on.
  • Payment: All groups are paid after the performance, not before.
