# Brag Plan: 3CDC Band Advance

## What is this app?
A self-serve show-advancing system for 3CDC's venues: a band fills out one form and the system auto-fills the day-sheet, drafts a venue-correct advance email, tracks the whole lifecycle, and files everything — replacing a stack of hand-typed Word docs.

## The angle
The anti-glamour brag. This isn't a flashy consumer app — it's the unsexy back-office grind of live-event production (advancing every band, every venue, every week) quietly automated end to end. The fun is the contrast: a genuinely boring problem killed by genuinely serious plumbing. Polished and dry-warm, never hype. Let the specificity ("day-sheet auto-filled," "follows up at 7 days out," "a human approves every send") do the bragging.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The actual band-facing form on the dark #0d1117 UI — header **"Band Advance / Show Details"** — with a cursor moving to the **"Submit show details"** button. Overlay line slams in and holds: **"A band fills out one form."** The hook is the promise that this one click does everything after it.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The submit **cascades**: three outputs arrive one by one — a **day-sheet auto-filling** (real checkboxes ticking), an **advance email drafted** (venue-correct), a **status log updating live**.
- The smart bits as clean cards: **"Knows a returning artist"** (6-month cross-venue lookback), **"Drafts 21 days out, nudges at 7,"** **"A human approves every send."**
- The **"Band Advance — Live"** dashboard: venue-color-coded show cards (FSQ blue / WP green) and a **"Needs you"** panel — mission control for the week.

## Outro / punchline
Name lockup: **3CDC Band Advance** with the line **"One form. Every venue. Nothing typed twice."**

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result:
1. Band opens the link, fills the form (bilingual EN/ES), clicks Submit.
2. The pipeline fans out — day-sheet filled, advance email drafted, status log stamped — with no one clicking anything.
3. Staff see it land on the live dashboard, color-coded by venue, flagged if it needs attention.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, confident internal-tools product film with a dry wink
- Interpretation: fewer scenes, longer holds, restrained motion; the energy comes from clean sequential reveals and one satisfying cascade, not from speed or flash.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm corporate bed with tasteful, motion-matched UI accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (120 BPM, clean/optimistic)
- Music treatment: start ~0s, sit low under the visuals, gentle fade-out over the last ~1.5s on the outro
- Music cue guidance: see section below
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the accent-blue elements breathe slightly with the bed; no waveform bars
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched — one click on Submit, three soft switch/tick sounds on the cascade arrivals, light card sounds on the feature reveals
- Audio-coupled moments: the Submit click, the three cascade outputs arriving one by one, the checkbox ticks on the day-sheet
- Restraint rule: no dense stingers, no comedic sfx, no beat-slamming text; polished > busy

## Music cue guidance
- Track: vol-1, ~120 BPM (preset available in `assets/music/cues/`).
- Target strong cues for major moments: **Submit click ~ align near a downbeat around 4.0s**; **cascade arrivals** step across the beat grid ~16.0s / 17.5s / 20.0s strong cues (or nearest equivalents once retimed to actual scene starts).
- Beat-grid windows: the three cascade outputs are a sequential reveal — snap to every other beat (~0.5–0.6s apart is fine for icon/label arrivals) but HOLD the full set on screen ~1s after the third lands so all three read.
- Restraint note: polished tone — use cues as light timing hints, not hard hits.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The form (Hook) — 4s
Recreate the band-facing form on the dark UI: header **"Band Advance / Show Details,"** a small **"Español"** toggle top-right, a couple of real fields visible (Show Basics; Stage & Technical; a "Stage plot" upload row), and the accent-blue **"Submit show details"** button. A cursor glides to the button and clicks. Overlay line holds center-lower: **"A band fills out one form."**
Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor moves to and clicks the Submit button (click SFX).
Audio intent: music enters warm and low; the click is the first deliberate accent.
Audio-coupled idea: simulated cursor click on Submit.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The cascade (Reveal) — 5.5s
From the Submit click, three output cards fan out one by one on the dark canvas, each with an icon and label:
1. **"Day-sheet — auto-filled"** — a small Word-doc card with three checkboxes visibly ticking ☒.
2. **"Advance email — venue-correct, drafted"** — an email card, accent-blue header.
3. **"Status log — updated live"** — a row stamping green.
Hold all three on screen together ~1s after the third lands. This is the centerpiece — the product doing its thing.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 cards arrive one by one, ~0.5–0.6s apart, each with a soft switch/tick; checkboxes tick on card 1.
Audio intent: three satisfying, restrained arrivals; a small sense of momentum.
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card reveal + checkbox ticks.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The smart bits (Highlights) — 5s
Three clean feature cards, polished app-store style, revealed in sequence and then held:
- **"Knows a returning artist"** — sub: 6-month cross-venue lookback.
- **"Drafts 21 days out. Nudges at 7."**
- **"A human approves every send."**
Each line short; hold each to its reading floor, keep the set on screen at the end.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 cards slide/settle one by one, light card sound each.
Audio intent: measured confidence; the middle of the arc.
Audio-coupled idea: sequential card reveal.
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The dashboard (Result) — 4s
Recreate **"Band Advance — Live"**: a grid of show cards color-coded by venue (FSQ dark blue, WP dark green, ~50% opacity fills), and a **"Needs you"** panel top-right with one highlighted item. Feels like a control board for the week. Brief cursor drift, no click needed.
Sequential/interaction: cards can settle in quickly as a set; the "Needs you" item pulses once.
Audio intent: the bed opens up slightly — the payoff view.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle presence swell under the dashboard.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 2.5s
Center lockup on near-black: **3CDC Band Advance**, accent-blue underline, tagline beneath: **"One form. Every venue. Nothing typed twice."** Music fades out.
Sequential/interaction: none — clean hold.
Audio intent: gentle resolve + fade.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Transition mood: fade to end.
